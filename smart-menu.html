    Smart Menu

    Introduction

    The Smart Menu is an innovative online ordering solution designed to streamline and automate the ordering process for restaurants. Whether customers are dining in, picking up, or opting for delivery, the Smart Menu allows them to order and pay on their own, enhancing convenience and efficiency.

    Key Benefits

    • Contactless, secure, and fast ordering process
    • Reduces wait times and staff dependency
    • Available 24/7 via mobile device
    • Supports seamless dine-in, pickup, and delivery modes

    Components

    Web Mobile Application

    Customers can easily browse the restaurant’s menu, place their orders, and make payments directly from any mobile device. This user-friendly interface ensures a seamless ordering experience whether they’re dining in or on the go.

    Back-Office Management

    Restaurant administrators can customize menus, manage products, and update prices in real time through a web-based dashboard. This application allows efficient control and smooth operations management.

    Kitchen Application

    This system ensures orders go directly from the customer to the kitchen, displayed in real-time. It eliminates communication gaps and accelerates food preparation while improving order accuracy.

    Smart Automation Integration

    The Smart Menu integrates with the POS system, ensuring synchronized order processing, inventory tracking, and financial reporting. This automation reduces human error and improves service speed.

    Core Features

    Ordering Methods

    • Dine-in via table QR
    • Pickup at counter
    • Home delivery with location tracking

    Multi-Language Support

    The Smart Menu interface supports both Arabic and English, ensuring accessibility and ease of use for diverse customer bases.

    Payment Integration

    • Credit/Debit cards
    • Apple Pay / Samsung Pay
    • Cash on delivery (if allowed)

    Menu Customization

    Admins can create multiple menu layouts, set product modifiers, create combo meals, or promote limited-time offers directly through the dashboard.

    Use Cases

    Dine-In

    Customers scan a table QR code to access the menu, place an order, and pay without leaving their seat.

    Pickup

    Users order in advance via the Smart Menu and pick up their meals when ready—no queues, no delays.

    Delivery

    Customers order from anywhere and get food delivered to their location, with integrated driver coordination and address management.
